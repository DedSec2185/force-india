From Concept to Highway in 5 Steps

How We Build
Your Custom Fleet.

Direct engineer-to-operator collaboration with full CAD transparency before a single plate of ST-52 steel is cut.

Step 01 · Day 1–2

Cargo & Route Audit

We analyse your cargo envelope, concentrated point loads, prime mover fifth-wheel height, and corridor bridge/turning limits.

Step 02 · Day 3–6

3D CAD & FEA Sign-Off

You receive complete General Arrangement (GA) drawings, axle-load distribution charts, and 2.5g FEA stress simulations for approval.

Step 03 · Week 2–3

Robotic SAW Fabrication

CNC plasma cutting, submerged-arc main beam welding, and 100% ultrasonic weld testing in our heavy fabrication bay.

Step 04 · Week 4

SA 2.5 Blast & OEM Fit

White-metal grit blasting, 140μm custom RAL polyurethane livery, WABCO EBS braking, JOST gear, and laser axle alignment.

Step 05 · Dispatch

ARAI Form-22A & Handover

Load-bay inspection, AIS-093 certification pack, and pan-India delivery to your depot or port gate.
